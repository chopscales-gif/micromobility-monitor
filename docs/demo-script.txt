MICROMOBILITY MONITOR
Two-minute demo script

Everything shown is simulated. The three operators are fictional and the software
is not used by any city. Say so at the start.

Before you begin: the seat picker is at the top right of every screen. Start on the
Compliance analyst seat, light theme, at the home screen.


0:00  Open

Say: "A city permits scooter and bike companies, and then has to take their word
for how many vehicles are out, where they are, and what they owe. This reads the
operators' own data feeds and checks all three. It is a demonstration on simulated
data, with three made-up operators."


0:10  Home, as the compliance analyst

Show: the home screen. Point at the first panel, Disputes to decide.

Say: "Each person opens on their own work, in the order it is best done. The
analyst has a dispute with the city's own deadline running, replies to read, and
findings nobody has told the operator about yet."


0:25  A case and its evidence

Click: case C-00043 at the top.

Show: the operator's dispute on the right and the escalation ladder, then scroll
down to the findings and their evidence.

Say: "A case is one operator, one rule, one week. Every finding carries its
evidence: which vehicles, where they stood, for how long. The operator disputed
it, and the city has five working days to uphold or withdraw."


0:45  The live map

Click: Live map in the menu. Switch to Density, then press Replay the last 24 hours
for a few seconds.

Say: "This is every vehicle on the street right now, from the feeds. The dashed
line is the permitted area, the shaded areas are the zones the city has drawn."


1:00  The money

Switch seat to Priya Raman, Finance. Click Invoices, then MM-2026-10-PRO.

Say: "The invoice is calculated from the trip records themselves, then set beside
what the operator reported. Where the two disagree by more than the tolerance, it
says so before the invoice goes out. Finance issues invoices; finance does not set
the fees. No seat can do everything."


1:20  The operator's side

Switch seat to Kestrel Mobility, Operator portal. Click Compliance.

Say: "The operator signs in to the same system and sees only its own records: how
its fleet measures against each rule this minute, and what the next notice would
cost. It cannot see another operator, or a case the city has not sent."


1:40  The public side

Click: Public open-data page, at the bottom of the menu. Scroll to the map.

Say: "And this is what the public gets: counts only, for finished months. Any
count under ten rides is withheld and places are rounded, so no single ride can be
picked out."


1:55  Close

Say: "Every read and every change in the console is in an audit log, and the
controls are written up plainly, including what is not built. This is a
demonstration: sign-in, email and real operator feeds are the work of connecting
it to a real program."


If you have another minute

  Data health (Program manager seat): one operator's feed is failing, and findings
  made while it was failing are held, not counted.

  Zones (Program manager seat): draw a new no-parking zone on the map and adopt it.

  Audit log (Program manager or IT administrator seat): the clicks you just made.

After the demo, put back what you changed. On a copy started with DEMO_RESET=1, use
"Reset it now" in the strip at the top of the console: it restores the zones, rules,
fee schedules, invoices and cases, and empties the audit log. Otherwise:

  npm run db:seed -- --violations

which rebuilds the cases, findings, alerts and outbox only.
